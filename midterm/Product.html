Mega T+ Inverter 10 CO

Rs 78,999

Description

The Mega T+ Inverter 10 CO is an inverter split air conditioner made for hot summers. Its inverter compressor changes speed instead of switching on and off again and again, so the room stays at a steady temperature and the electricity bill stays lower.

4D Airflow moves the air left and right and also up and down, so the cool air reaches every corner of the room instead of one spot. This is useful in larger bedrooms and lounges where one fixed direction leaves some places warm.

The Memory Resumption feature remembers the last settings of the AC. After a power cut, the AC starts again with the same mode and temperature, so you do not have to set it again with the remote every time the light comes back.

Gen Mode lets you choose the current level the AC is allowed to draw. When you run the house on a generator or a small UPS, this helps you control the power use so the AC does not overload it.

The 6-in-1 health filters help to keep the air in the room cleaner. Dust and small particles are caught by the filters, which is good for families with children or people who are sensitive to dust.

Zone Follow uses a sensor in the remote control. The AC then cools by the real temperature around the person holding the remote and not only by the temperature near the indoor unit.

Auto cleaning of the AC interior removes dust from the inside of the indoor unit by itself. This keeps the cooling strong for longer and reduces the number of times the unit needs to be cleaned by hand.

The AC comes with a 12 years compressor warranty, 6 years warranty on functional parts and free installation. Installments are available, and you can get an additional 2.5% off if you pay by card.
